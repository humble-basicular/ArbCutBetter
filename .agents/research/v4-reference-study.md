## Later research — user direction, 5 November

Design depends on the niche. In the current synthesis, distinguish broadly useful design principles from choices whose suitability depends on the trade, audience and service. A successful-looking construction or HVAC site does not automatically establish the right visual treatment for arborist work. No conversion success is established without supporting evidence.

After the current study, identify niches the reference set missed and suggest two comparable reference sites for each additional niche for the user to look over. **Present the candidate sites first; wait for the user's approval before analysing them.** This is a later phase, not permission to expand the current research now. The missing niches and candidate sites have not yet been selected.

## Provisional whole-site rules from the Puetz pass

These are design judgements supported by Puetz evidence, awaiting comparison with other US sites; no conversion result is established.

| Page type | Transferable rule | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Home/service | Keep a recognisable visual system; change photos, questions and proof to fit the service. Limit repeated promotional tails on mobile. | [Inner services](v4-reference-study/puetz-inner-pages.md), [service variations](v4-reference-study/puetz-services-continuation.md) |
| Locality | Change relevant evidence and local facts throughout the page; changing the city in a headline is insufficient. | [Commercial/decks examples](v4-reference-study/puetz-gap-closure.md), [siding examples](v4-reference-study/puetz-final-sampling.md) |
| Gallery/reviews | Make work and source-marked testimony the main content; retain readable context and avoid endless mobile cards. | [Projects/reviews](v4-reference-study/puetz-inner-pages.md) |
| About/team | Named people, roles and consistent portraits supply specificity; put useful facts before long values statements. | [Leadership](v4-reference-study/puetz-remaining-types.md) |
| Contact/FAQ | Make details and answers easy to reach; preserve usable mobile controls without floating UI covering content. | [Contact/FAQ](v4-reference-study/puetz-inner-pages.md), [limited live FAQ](v4-reference-study/puetz-gap-closure.md) |
| Articles | Reuse a calm reading shell; choose checklists, steps, comparisons and photographs to match the answer. Keep biographies from delaying it. | [Six article examples across two batches](v4-reference-study/puetz-final-sampling.md), [earlier comparison](v4-reference-study/puetz-articles-localities.md) |
| Legal | Prioritise a readable text column, clear headings and contact route; customer-sales sections add little to the task. | [Legal pages](v4-reference-study/puetz-remaining-types.md) |
|404 | Explain the missing page and offer one prominent recovery action plus useful destinations. Verify actual404response separately. | [Actual404probe](v4-reference-study/puetz-remaining-types.md) |
| Prices/success | I do not know: no verified public service-price or success-page reference from Puetz. Financing is not transparent pricing; no submission was made. | [Financing](v4-reference-study/puetz-inner-pages.md) |

Puetz's particular navy/red/tan, construction drawings, ribbons, supplier set and regional copy are trade/brand choices, not a visual prescription for arborists. Shared type/colour/photo treatment creates coherence; repeated identical page composition does not establish quality. Later sources must test these provisional rules.
