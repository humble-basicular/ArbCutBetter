Eighteen additional interaction screenshots cover projects, filters, contact and navigation at 1440/390/360; the 360 menu-open attempt did not visibly open the menu. [Exact URL ledger](v4-reference-study/puetz-ledger.json).

The strongest provisional whole-site lesson is a consistent visual vocabulary with a composition that fits each page's job: service photos and a form for services; large work images for projects; source-marked testimony for reviews; named people and real artefacts for About; direct contact details beside the form; readable topic-grouped FAQ. Puetz retains navy/red/tan, display typography, drawing textures, ribbons and crew imagery while changing layout. Repeating one homepage structure is not what makes the site coherent. These are reasoned observations from one US site, still to test against the other references.

Polish does not excuse weak information: very long mobile review cards, repetitive service-page tails, unrelated project imagery, dropped mobile footer link columns and overlapping chat UI are defects to avoid. Financing supplies no useful price comparison; a strong public-price page reference remains unknown.

## Later research — user direction, 5 November

Design depends on the niche. In the current synthesis, distinguish broadly useful design principles from choices whose suitability depends on the trade, audience and service. A successful-looking construction or HVAC site does not automatically establish the right visual treatment for arborist work. No conversion success is established without supporting evidence.

After the current study, identify niches the reference set missed and suggest two comparable reference sites for each additional niche for the user to look over. **Present the candidate sites first; wait for the user's approval before analysing them.** This is a later phase, not permission to expand the current research now. The missing niches and candidate sites have not yet been selected.
