## USA evidence: Puetz, partial

[Eight complete inner pages and their design findings](v4-reference-study/puetz-inner-pages.md): roofing, windows/doors, projects, reviews, about, contact, FAQ and financing. Both desktop and mobile were inspected through the footer: **16 page/width records, 47 document segments and 16 first viewports actually viewed**. Eighteen additional interaction screenshots cover projects, filters, contact and navigation at 1440/390/360; the 360 menu-open attempt did not visibly open the menu. [Exact URL ledger](v4-reference-study/puetz-ledger.json).

The strongest provisional whole-site lesson is a consistent visual vocabulary with a composition that fits each page's job: service photos and a form for services; large work images for projects; source-marked testimony for reviews; named people and real artefacts for About; direct contact details beside the form; readable topic-grouped FAQ. Puetz retains navy/red/tan, display typography, drawing textures, ribbons and crew imagery while changing layout. Repeating one homepage structure is not what makes the site coherent. These are reasoned observations from one US site, still to test against the other references.

Polish does not excuse weak information: very long mobile review cards, repetitive service-page tails, unrelated project imagery, dropped mobile footer link columns and overlapping chat UI are defects to avoid. Financing supplies no useful price comparison; a strong public-price page reference remains unknown.

A further scripted batch captured 17 URLs at both widths, including blog, two articles, two localities, remaining services and utility pages. These **34 page/width records remain unviewed**. They do not expand the analysed count. Next is Puetz `/blog/`, its two captured articles, then `/roofing/winona/` and `/roofing/rochester/`; compare actual templates before setting represented-by mappings. Prior homepage evidence is reused, not included in fresh counts. Other US sites retain the prior partial states in the [inherited coverage table](v4-reference-study/vensland-baseline.md#exact-coverage-ledger). Glassperts still needs a successful load.
