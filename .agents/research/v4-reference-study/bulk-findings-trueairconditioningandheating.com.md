- **Real crew/work as primary proof.** Bright people/dog/equipment hero and later branded work photographs make the business identifiable. For ArbCut, use its own team and tree work, preserving the subject at mobile width. `S/home/1440/first-viewport.jpg`, y=0; `S/cooling-services__cuero-tx/1440/segment-002400.jpg`, y=2400–4800.
- **One restrained frame/overlap motif.** Blue photo edge and offset white form give hierarchy without extra decorative badges. Adapt sparingly with ArbCut's palette. `S/home/1440/segment-002400.jpg`, y=2400–4800.
- **Unequal service hierarchy.** Home's tall installation and wide repair tiles communicate priorities better than six identical cards. Retain the hierarchy while reducing overlay darkness. `S/home/1440/segment-000000.jpg`, y=0–2400.

Use only verified ArbCut evidence and current consistent ratings. `S/ductwork/1440/first-viewport.jpg`, y=0; `S/cooling-services__cuero-tx/1440/segment-000000.jpg`, y=0–2400.
