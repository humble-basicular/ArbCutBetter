## Copy — design judgments, provisional at sheet scale
- Keep a clear colour hierarchy and a light reading surface: blue actions/bands, slate and charcoal support, largely white page. Evidence: `_sheets/veteranscontractingllc.com/1440/t06-_1-0.jpg` c1–8, whole cells. Transfer the consistency, not this brand's exact palette.
- Lead inner service pages with a specific service title and large relevant work photograph rather than decorative pictograms. Evidence: `1440/t06-_1-0.jpg` c2/c4/c5/c6/c8 top third; mobile equivalents `390/t06-_1-0.jpg` same cells.
- Make financing a clear optional path and quote a dedicated short route. Evidence: `1440/t06-_1-0.jpg` c3 top/blue middle band and `390/t07-_1-0.jpg` c1 title/map region. This is a layout lesson, not a recommendation to invent ArbCut financing.
- Preserve useful footer navigation on mobile. Evidence: `390/t10-location_x-0.jpg` c1–2 footer visibly retains service/resource columns; clicking, semantics and target correctness untested.

## Avoid — design judgments and unresolved warning signs
- Long equal-weight service stacks and exhaustive crew runs: `390/t06-_1-0.jpg` c7 middle service stack; `390/t09-locations_x-0.jpg` c4/c6 dark crew run. ArbCut needs hierarchy and concise navigation, not copied page length.
- Treating generic repeated photographs as local proof: `1440/t08-locations_x-0.jpg` c1–6 hero uses the same image; `1440/t09-locations_x-0.jpg` c1–6 secondary collage repeats. Location provenance unknown.
- Publishing mismatched locality copy or thin destination pages: `390/t04-locations_x-0.jpg` c2/c10/c18 title areas and `390/t10-location_x-0.jpg` c1–2 body above footer. These are candidates for verification here, but implementation should explicitly guard against these patterns.
- Copying article density or huge sparse archives: `390/t07-_1-0.jpg` c2/c7 long reading runs; `390/t03-2025_x-0.jpg` c1–4/c7–20 grid areas. Exact reading comfort and missing-media causes need full-size checks.
- Homemade proof badges, inferred ratings, decorative glyphs or invented proof copy: nothing in this milestone authenticates Veterans proof for reuse. The BBB mark is visible in several mobile footers; use only ArbCut's own substantiated official sources per v4 rules.
