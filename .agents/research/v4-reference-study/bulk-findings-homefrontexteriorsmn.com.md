## Copy / avoid for ArbCut

**Copy, as design judgments rather than conversion claims:**

1. One useful overlap: white form crossing dark hero boundary, but simplify its fields and maintain full labels (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/home/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y640–740). Make the mobile primary action reachable without reproducing the long stack.
2. Short named/source-linked review attached to the relevant work image (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/home/1440/segment-002400.jpg` y650–780); source linkage for ArbCut must be implemented explicitly, not assumed from this screenshot.
3. Six compact numbered service links with one expanded preview instead of six huge mobile cards (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/home/1440/segment-002400.jpg` y850–1840; `/390/segment-004220.jpg` and `segment-005064.jpg`). Interaction between tabs remains unverified.
4. One coherent accent and two repeated core motifs, restrained against large real photos (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/wood-decks/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y1100–2350).
5. Named staff in consistent real uniforms and a signed owner statement, with actual work photos (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/about/1440/segment-002400.jpg` y100–1500; `home/1440/segment-004800.jpg` y620–1180). Use real ArbCut people and claims.
6. Visible regional links beside useful map content; preserve equivalent links in mobile footer too (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/home/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y800–1250; `drone-thermal-scan/390/segment-005908.jpg` y0–200).
7. Manufacturer credential beside the explanation of its practical value, only when genuine and evidenced (`homefrontexteriorsmn.com/brava-roofing-contractor-minnesota/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y1800–2250).

**Avoid:**

1. Slogan-only home H1 and tiny generic inner H1 while larger H2 does the visual work; blog titles with no H1 (`home/1440/first-viewport.jpg`; `about/1440/record.json`, `blog__guide-to-choose-shingle-color/1440/record.json`). This is semantic observation, not a claimed ranking penalty.
2. Decorative glyph pictograms/arrows/stars, invented guarantee badges or imported stock credibility (`home/1440/segment-004800.jpg` y620–1180; `trex-decking/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y1200–2350). Actual Google/product logos are a different category; provenance still needs evidence.
3. Inconsistent proof/commercial copy: hero 5.0 vs map 4.9 (114), home $200,000 vs financing $150K (`home/1440/segment-000000.jpg` y540–590,820–910; `home/1440/segment-004800.jpg` y150–350; `financing/1440/first-viewport.jpg` y605–660).
