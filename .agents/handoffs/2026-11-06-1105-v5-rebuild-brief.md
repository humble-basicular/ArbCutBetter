## What makes those sites work (lead's notes; use these, not the old brain's restraint)
Full and layered screens; real company in the picture; characterful logos; a visual world of their own (Puetz Minnesota and blueprint line art, Genz sunburst and halftone, GreenTek grid, Home Front red square between offset photos); committed saturated colour bands; crafted headlines with voice (light tracked line then huge bold, hand-drawn underline, highlighted word, tilted label) and named things ("Puetz Promise", "HomePulse", "Customer For Life Mindset"); proof everywhere; custom icons; small human touches (speech bubble with a face, rep cards, form tabs such as "Ready To Get Started?"); visible finish (shadows, depth, overlapping photos, utility bar above the header); a sales-page spine (hero and form, proof, services, process, owner, reviews, area, CTA).

## How v4 failed (do not repeat)
Called Genz "busy" and capped devices at two; banned decoration; pale tints with orange on buttons only; stars replaced by "5 iš 5"; icons replaced by text and the planned raster set never made; one font, one weight, in a wide flat style that is itself the current AI look; flat white card after flat white card; proof as a logo beside loose text; rough phone-photo cut-out with a green halo; logos straight from image prompts; a CSS ring diagram invented as a map; studied screenshots only, so no real motion. The rules in `arbcut-design-brain.md` Part B and C caused much of this: rewrite them. Part A2 (never-ship defects) and A4 (mobile acceptance) still stand.
